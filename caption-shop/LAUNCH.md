## Product and target customer

An original, downloadable offline subtitle utility for freelance video editors and creators who already have SRT or VTT captions. Version 1.0 converts subtitles and transcripts, applies timing offsets, wraps lines, flags possible readability problems and exports batches as ZIPs. It does not perform audio transcription, translation or speech correction. A self-contained browser file reduces recurring processing and hosting costs.

This is a testable product hypothesis. There is no evidence yet of willingness to pay, customer demand or a conversion rate. Free caption tools compete with it; the proposed value is a simple offline batch workflow with several export formats and explicit review checks. Do not describe it as proven income or guaranteed daily earnings.

**Description:**

Make the last step of your caption workflow easier. Caption Cleanup Kit is a small offline workspace for existing SRT and WebVTT files.

Convert between SRT and VTT; export plain transcripts, timestamped transcripts or CSV cue sheets; shift all timings by a chosen offset; wrap text at word boundaries; tidy spaces and optionally remove formatting tags. Select up to 100 files and download one ZIP containing the converted files and a review report. Limits are 2 MB per file and 20 MB per batch.

Your download contains a self-contained HTML toolkit, a quick-start guide and original SRT/VTT examples. Open the toolkit in a current desktop browser. Nothing to install, no account needed for the workspace and no cloud uploads.

The kit edits existing subtitles. It does not generate captions from audio, translate speech or check accuracy against media. WebVTT notes, styles, regions, identifiers and positioning settings are omitted. Review exported files before publishing. Readability flags are editing prompts, not an accessibility certification.

One purchaser may use the kit for personal and commercial client projects. Your own exports are yours to use when you hold the necessary rights.
