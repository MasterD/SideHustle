Before you buy

What do I need?

A desktop computer, a current browser and an existing UTF-8 SRT or VTT file. Your editor or transcription service provides the initial captions.

Can I work offline?

Yes. After downloading and extracting the ZIP, open the toolkit HTML file on your device. It includes everything needed to process subtitle text.

Does it generate captions from audio?

No. It cleans and reformats existing subtitle files. It does not upload media or use a transcription API.

Will it preserve styling?

It preserves caption text and timings. WebVTT positioning, styles, regions, notes and identifiers are omitted. Formatting tags can be retained for subtitle exports by changing an option.

Can I use it for client work?

Yes. One purchaser can use the kit for personal and commercial projects and share their own exports. The toolkit itself cannot be redistributed or resold.
