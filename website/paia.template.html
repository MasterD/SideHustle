Getting guidance or requesting a record

The Regulator's guide explains access, assistance, fees, refusals and remedies. Its English guide and Afrikaans guide are available online. Ask the business contact for help obtaining or inspecting a copy; other language versions and current forms are on the official PAIA page.

For a formal request, send the completed Form 2 to the contact above. Identify the record, the right you wish to exercise or protect, why that record is required and the preferred access method. Provide necessary authority if acting for another person. The proprietor verifies identity and authority proportionately and applies the statutory process. Listing records here does not grant unrestricted access to confidential information.

Any prescribed request/access fee and applicable exemption is assessed under current rules and communicated using the prescribed process, including Form 3. Requests receive a decision within the applicable statutory period, generally 30 days, subject to a lawful extension. A refusal or failure to respond can be taken to the Regulator through Form 5 or the applicable court process. This private body has no compulsory internal appeal procedure. Contact the Regulator at enquiries@inforegulator.org.za or 010 023 5200 for official assistance.

Personal information in this operation

Business purposes are answering requested enquiries, assessing and delivering agreed work, keeping necessary financial evidence and handling rights requests. Data subjects may include the proprietor, enquirers, customer contacts and people lawfully included in an agreed export. Necessary categories are contact details, correspondence, scope/acceptance evidence and payment records. Begin with anonymised examples; production-data handling is agreed before transfer. Medical records, payroll and credentials are outside the first pilot.

Gmail processes business email; Cloudflare Pages is the approved public host and handles website delivery/security information. These global providers may process information in the United States or other countries under their terms. Production transfers need a separate lawful arrangement. Necessary disclosure may be made to the relevant customer, the bank, SARS, a regulator or authorised advisers if engaged. Information is not supplied to a marketing list.

Operating procedures require controlled device/account access, minimal data, appropriate protection, dated local backups and agreed deletion of customer working files. The proprietor verifies controls before handling production data. Unsuccessful enquiries follow the privacy notice's 90-day review/deletion plan unless a lawful retention reason applies. Statutory evidence is kept for its applicable period. Access, correction, objection and deletion requests go to the business contact; lawful retention duties can limit deletion.
