- Do you currently accept new resellers and ship individual orders directly to their customers?

- What is the complete per-order cost, including pick/pack, delivery, any service fee and any subscription? Can a bundle ship in one parcel?
- Which Shopify integration supports live stock, paid-order import and tracking? Please link the official app listing and current integration documentation.
- Can supplier payment and fulfilment proceed automatically? Is it funded by a balance, saved payment method or credit account? What happens if funds are insufficient? Are there transaction limits?
- What prevents duplicate orders and how are stock-outs, cancellations, failed payments and failed deliveries handled?
- What are dispatch and delivery times, return address, defect/sizing return terms and refund timelines?
- May we use your product photos and descriptions in our store and advertising? Is packaging unbranded?
- Can we review the catalogue and full agreement without paying, and what would a sample order cost?

This is an information request, not a purchase commitment.
