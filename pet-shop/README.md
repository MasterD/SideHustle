## What must work before checkout opens

1. Approved supplier account, exact sellable SKUs, legitimate image rights, quoted landed costs, stock feed, direct customer shipping and written return terms.
2. Approved budget and store access. Owner completes identity, banking and new-credential entry privately.
3. Configure payment confirmation, order routing, supplier billing, tracking and customer notifications with hosted integrations. A local Codex check is not an order fulfilment service.
4. Verify a permitted test order from checkout to supplier acknowledgement, dispatch and tracking. Test stock-out behaviour and cancellation; confirm duplicate events cannot create duplicate purchases.
5. Establish cash reserve and refunds process. Keep ads off until unit economics are positive and spending is explicitly approved.
