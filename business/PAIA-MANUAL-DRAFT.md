## Personal-information processing

Purpose: answer requested enquiries, assess suitability, agree and deliver the service, issue invoices, retain required evidence and handle rights requests. Categories: the owner, enquirers, customer contacts and people whose data is lawfully included in an agreed customer export. Typical information includes contact details, correspondence, scope and payment/acceptance evidence. Avoid unnecessary personal data; start with synthetic or anonymised samples. Medical, payroll and credential handling are outside the first pilot.

The business mailbox uses Gmail; the website's hosting provider handles technical delivery/security data once hosting is approved. International provider processing must be reviewed under the applicable POPIA requirements. Production-data provider access and transfers need a written arrangement before files are shared. Local financial evidence remains separately retained.

Planned safeguards: owner-controlled device and accounts, appropriate access protection, minimal access, no shared passwords, local dated backups, an audit-preserving financial ledger and agreed deletion of working customer exports. Monitor unsuccessful enquiries weekly and apply the published 90-day deletion plan unless a lawful retention reason exists. Review incidents promptly and follow applicable notification duties. These controls are planned procedures; the owner verifies their actual operation.
