## Income tax and provisional tax

For the proposed sole proprietorship, business income belongs in the owner's ITR12 alongside salary and other income. Deductibility and taxable profit need review; the dashboard's cash surplus is not the taxable figure. [SARS sole proprietor guidance](https://www.sars.gov.za/businesses-and-employers/small-businesses-taxpayers/starting-a-business-and-tax/sole-proprietorship/)

Business income can bring provisional-tax obligations. Provisional tax is advance payment of income tax, using IRP6 estimates. The owner should review estimated total taxable income, PAYE, rebates, credits and prior payments through official SARS channels before trading. For a February year end, SARS specifies August's last applicable business day and February's last business day for the two payments. The second 2027-year payment date is 26 February 2027 under that rule (28 February is a Sunday). The earlier 31 August 2026 date is relevant only if an obligation already existed; starting preparations now does not by itself establish a past liability. [SARS provisional tax](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/provisional-tax/)

The 2027 individual tax year runs from 1 March 2026 to 28 February 2027. Income-tax return submission dates are separate from IRP6 payments; confirm the applicable filing notice rather than assuming they coincide. Current 2026 filing dates relate to the prior assessment year. [SARS filing season](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/filing-season/)

The reserve percentage in the monitor is deliberately unset. A flat percentage of positive cash surplus is only a savings plan. A later estimate requires total income and PAYE, privately supplied to the owner or a registered practitioner. No ID number, SARS password or tax reference is needed in chat.

## VAT

As administered by SARS from 1 April 2026, the compulsory threshold is R2.3 million of taxable supplies in any consecutive 12 months. Written contractual obligations can also trigger registration. SARS states a 21-business-day application window once liability arises. Its Budget FAQs describe the legislative status of the announced changes; recheck before a filing decision. [SARS register for VAT](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/value-added-tax/register-for-vat/), [Budget FAQs](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)

Do not charge VAT or use a VAT registration number while unregistered. If registration becomes necessary, review pricing and invoice requirements before quoting. The monitor aggregates the three offer categories as one seller and shows recorded cash receipts as a preliminary warning; unpaid invoices, contracted supplies, other activities and adjustments can make actual VAT turnover different. It is not a complete VAT calculation. Gross expenses are recorded without calculating input-tax recovery.

## Do not elect turnover tax automatically

Low revenue does not guarantee eligibility. SARS's turnover-tax FAQs include information technology and consulting in professional services, which are subject to exclusion tests. Review eligibility and consequences before any election; ordinary sole-proprietor income-tax records are the starting plan. [SARS turnover-tax FAQs](https://www.sars.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Ops/Guides/Legal-Pub-FAQs-TT01-FAQs-on-Turnover-Tax.pdf)

## Evidence and reconciliation

Keep dated quotes, accepted scopes, sequential invoices, bank statements, fee/expense receipts, refund documents, delivery acceptance and relevant SARS records. Retain records generally for five years from return submission, with longer retention where required for unfiled returns, audits or disputes. Keep originals organised and accessible. Check authorisation requirements before moving statutory electronic records outside South Africa. [SARS recordkeeping](https://www.sars.gov.za/client-segments/record-keeping/)

Monthly owner review: match every bank movement to an invoice, expense, refund, tax payment or owner transfer; record gross sales and separately withheld provider fees; verify cash received against bank deposits; review unpaid invoices, customer deposits and delivery liabilities; confirm supporting filenames point to retained files; export records and make a dated database/evidence backup. Track personal withdrawals separately from operating expenses. Do not label every cost deductible without checking its purpose and evidence.

Start with South African clients and ZAR invoices. Foreign customers or merchant-of-record platforms require a separate currency, cross-border VAT and settlement review before use. This first tool does not handle those cases.
