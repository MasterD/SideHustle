Objective: one properly scoped, fully paid R3,500 reporting pilot. This is a validation target, not a revenue forecast. Current revenue and customer count are zero. The first R1,750 deposit creates a delivery/refund obligation; it is not all freely available profit.

Audience: South African agency owners and small service teams who rebuild the same weekly sales or outstanding-invoice report from CSV exports. Remote delivery; no city restriction. One active pilot fits the three-hour weekly owner budget.

## Qualification and next action

1. Confirm the person wants to discuss the report; record the source/date and permitted channel in the monitor's consent note.
2. Confirm one recurring workflow, no more than two usable exports and up to five agreed measures. Ask about required computer setup and the person who can verify results.
3. Check that the scope and delivery slot fit the owner's actual available time. Quote only one active pilot.
4. Agree data handling before receiving even an anonymised sample. Verify a sample output against expected totals.
5. Fill the seller disclosures and final quote; approve it before sending. Use a unique quote/invoice reference and the locally verified FNB EFT instructions.
6. Confirm cleared funds in the bank statement before starting paid work. Record actual money in the ledger; record unearned quoted amounts only in the pipeline.

## This week's owner time budget

Allocate up to 60 minutes to required owner setup/review, 15 minutes to the approved public post, 30 minutes to up to two discovery conversations, 15 minutes to records and 60 minutes to delivery review/buffer. If setup takes longer, reduce that week's discovery/delivery commitments; do not exceed three hours by promising an early slot.

Review after five suitable conversations. If there is no willingness to pay, change the customer problem or offer before expanding. Do not add advertising, domains or subscriptions to solve unvalidated demand.

The remaining follow-ups are replies to requested information or documented consent, not a mass-email campaign. The Regulator's guidance also covers unsolicited LinkedIn direct messages.
